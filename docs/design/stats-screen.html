Stats screen, in the Big Keys style

Two views: a player's own stats, and a household overview for comparing players. Phones are drawn at full scroll length; the blue line marks the end of the first screen (844 px). Period: the last 12 weeks. The data is made up but adds up.

Design notes

  • Same look as Big Keys. The S/D/T chips use the same colours as the game pad, so "D" is green everywhere in the app.
  • One headline number (win ratio) with the change from the previous period. Everything else is one scroll away.
  • The dart mix is labelled rows, not a pie. Doubles and triples are small shares (6–9%), and rows make them easy to compare and show the change.
  • Three small trend charts instead of one chart with two scales. Weekly points smooth out one bad evening. Tap or drag a chart to read a week.
  • Head to head = "finished ahead of" in games both played, so it works for 3+ player games and Killer, not just 1-vs-1.
  • The grid suits up to about 6 players. Past that, the household view falls back to a list per player.

To decide

  • Kids vs adults. A child will usually be last on household rankings. Should the household view lead with each player's own improvement instead of the ranking?
  • Guests. The same screens can work from local data, so guests get stats on their own device too.
